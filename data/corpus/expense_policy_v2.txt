Northbeam Snacks Inc — Expense Policy, Version 2, effective March 1, 2025
Expense Policy Version 2 supersedes Version 1 dated January 15, 2024. Under Version 2, the daily meal allowance for business travel is $75 per employee. Travel requests require approval from a director. Reimbursement claims must be submitted within 15 days of the expense. Hotel stays are capped at $220 per night under Version 2.
